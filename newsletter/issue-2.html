Deal breakdown: lower rate or fewer points? (EXAMPLE numbers)

These are made-up quotes to show the math. They aren't offers from any lender.

Same $175,000 loan, interest only, two quotes:

Quote A Quote B
Rate 9.00% 10.50%
Points 2.0 ($3,500) 0.5 ($875)
Interest over 6 months $7,875 $9,188
Total cost at 6 months $11,375 $10,063

Quote A has the lower rate and still costs about $1,300 more on a six-month flip. The extra 1.5 points ($2,625) take 12 months of lower interest ($219 a month) to pay back. Unless you'll hold the loan longer than a year, the "worse" rate is the better deal.

Quick tip: ask for the experience tier, not the headline

Before you compare lenders, tell each one how many flips you've closed in the last 24 months and ask which pricing tier that puts you in. The headline rate is for their best tier. Comparing headline to headline when you're a first-time flipper is comparing two numbers you won't get.
